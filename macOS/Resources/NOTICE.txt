Hermex for Mac

Community macOS port of https://github.com/uzairansaruzi/hermex
Original Hermex copyright Uzair Ansar and contributors, MIT License (LICENSE.txt).
macOS port maintained in https://github.com/KenMercusLai/hermex-mac

The macOS target directly compiles the original platform-independent APIClient,
Endpoint definitions, models, multipart builder, catalog and usage policies from
HermesMobile. The SSE event decoder is extracted unchanged in UpstreamSSE.swift;
only the EventSource transport is replaced with Apple URLSession streaming.
UpstreamAdapters.swift retains small webui memory/bounded-download excerpts.
The app icon is the original Hermex light app icon, used from the MIT source tree.
The original iOS source, license, and Git history are retained.

No third-party Swift packages are linked into the macOS binary. SwiftUI, AppKit,
Foundation, Security, ImageIO and Charts are Apple frameworks. No hosted backend,
analytics SDK, agent runtime, model weights, push relay or credentials are bundled.

This community build is ad-hoc signed with hardened runtime. It is not signed by
Apple Developer ID, notarized, or distributed through the Mac App Store. macOS may
require explicit approval before opening a downloaded community build.
